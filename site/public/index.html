Nous Atlas

A free workbook for worldbuilding and notes. Lots of tabs of text, links between them, and maps you can paint layers onto. It opens and saves ordinary Excel (.xlsx) files. No subscription, ever.

You're on a phone or tablet. Nous Atlas is a program for a Windows, Mac or Linux computer. Open this page on your computer to download it: the address is nousatlas.lionthroat.com

Using Nous Atlas

Your sheets are listed on the left, grouped. Your file stays a normal .xlsx, so Excel and Google Sheets still open it.

Jump to a sheet

Press Ctrl+P and type part of its name. Alt+← goes back.

Find anything

Press Ctrl+F. It searches every sheet at once.

Follow a link

Links to other sheets show as buttons. Click one to go there.

Paint a map layer

On a map sheet, open Layers on the toolbar, make a layer, pick up the brush and drag over squares.

Hover a square

On a map, rest the mouse on a square to see the places, creatures and notes there.

Save

Press Ctrl+S. Changed your mind? Ctrl+Z undoes.

Questions

Why does my computer warn me about it?

Microsoft and Apple charge yearly fees for the certificates that make those warnings go away. Nous Atlas is free and made by one person, so it skips them. The warnings appear once; after that it opens like any other program.

Is it really free?

Yes, no trial, no subscription, no ads. It's open source: anyone can read exactly what it does on GitHub.

Will it change my Excel files?

Only when you save, and they stay ordinary .xlsx files. Atlas keeps its own extras (sheet groups, map layers) in a hidden sheet that Excel ignores. If a file has something Atlas can't keep, such as charts, it saves a copy instead of touching the original.

How do I get a newer version?

Download and install it the same way. It replaces the old one.

How do I uninstall it?

Windows: Settings → Apps → Installed apps → Nous Atlas → Uninstall. Mac: drag Nous Atlas from Applications to the Trash. Linux: remove “nous-atlas” in Software Manager, or sudo apt remove nous-atlas.
